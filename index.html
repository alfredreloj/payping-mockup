What the phone app does

  • Reads GCash notifications only. It ignores every other app — texts, chats, bank alerts are never touched.
  • Pulls out amount + reference number the moment a payment lands, and sends it to the store.
  • Stays alive in the background and pings the store every minute so they know it's working.
  • Saves the reference number when GCash includes it — used for exact matching and reconciliation.
